# daily-ai-digest

A running archive of daily AI and tech news digests, and the LinkedIn post published from each one.

Maintained for [Selvin PaulRaj K](https://linkedin.com/in/selvinpaulraj) (Applied AI Engineer, Chennai). One entry per publishing day.

## How it works

1. Trending AI and tech stories are collected each morning from X/Twitter, AI Weekly, TechCrunch, Reuters, vendor blogs and security feeds.
2. The 3 to 5 stories that actually matter to engineers building agents, LLM apps and RAG systems get picked. Funding noise and politics get dropped unless they change what engineers can ship.
3. One post is drafted in a human voice: a real hook, concrete numbers, an opinion, and a discussion question. No AI tells, no em dashes, no hype adjectives.
4. The post is published to LinkedIn, then archived here with its live URL and sources.

Post craft follows the conventions in [sergebulaev/linkedin-skills](https://github.com/sergebulaev/linkedin-skills): hook formulas, the humanizer pass, the post audit, and the 900 to 1300 character sweet spot.

## Layout

```
posts/YYYY-MM-DD.md    the published post, its LinkedIn URL, and the sources behind it
engagement/YYYY-MM.md  comment and reply passes, three a day
LOG.md                 one line per day, newest first
```
